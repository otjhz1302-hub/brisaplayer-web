Our ad promise

Brisa is free and supported by ads, with rules written into the app's code:

  • Your music is never paused, cut or lowered by an ad. Every ad is silent.
  • Your first week has no ads at all.
  • Banners stay in fixed spots. At most two full-screen ads a day, only when you close the player.
  • Never when you open the app or change songs. Never on your lock screen or in notifications.
  • No promotional notifications, no accounts, no tracking of your library.

Make it yours

Basic, Neon or Glass style, your own background photo, and animations you can turn on or off.

Private by design

Your library, playlists, history and settings never leave your phone. No sign-up. Brisa plays files you already have; it is not a downloader.
