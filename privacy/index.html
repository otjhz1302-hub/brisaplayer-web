Privacy policy

Brisa Music & Video Player · Otjh LLC · 2026-10-29

Summary

Brisa Player plays the music and videos that are already on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking by us. Your library, playlists, statistics, lyrics and settings never leave your device. The only data that leaves the phone is what the advertising partners below collect to show ads.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your music and video library, folders, playlists, queues, favorites, play history and the local "music recap".
  • Equalizer, theme, background photo and other settings; lyrics and tag edits; the private folder.
  • Backups you export are files you choose where to save. We never receive them.

We (Otjh LLC) do not collect, receive or store any of this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

  • Music and videos / storage: to find and play your media files.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep playing with the screen off.
  • Modify audio settings: for the equalizer and sound effects.
  • Display over other apps (only if you turn on the floating player) and modify system settings (only if you set a song as ringtone).
  • Internet and network state: for ads and for Wi-Fi Transfer on your local network.

Wi-Fi Transfer

When you open Wi-Fi Transfer, the app runs a small server on your local network, protected by a one-time code, so you can send files from your computer or another phone. Files go straight to your phone. Nothing passes through our servers, and the server stops when you leave that screen.

Advertising

Brisa is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network (through Google's mediation). The ad software does not run during your first week of use. When it runs, these partners may collect, automatically:

DataPurpose
IP address (used to estimate approximate location)Advertising, analytics of ad delivery, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads (views, taps)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (e.g. ad load performance)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device identifiers: advertising ID and app set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

Brisa does not give the advertising partners any information about your music, videos or how you use the player. Data is encrypted in transit. In regions that require it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), Brisa asks for your consent with Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps · Meta Privacy Policy.

Children

Brisa is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
  • You can change your ad consent in Brisa's Settings (where available).
  • Uninstalling Brisa removes everything the app stored on your phone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: otjhllc@gmail.com.
